My Story.

I am a Software Engineer and Data Analyst blending AI-driven innovation with operational resilience to build intelligent, scalable systems.

I'm Amir Tamang. My technical journey is anchored by a BSc in Computing and my current pursuit of a Master of Information Technology at Australian Catholic University (ACU) here in Sydney.

Unlike traditional paths, my experience bridges high-level technical architecture and high-pressure operational environments. I architect full-stack .NET enterprise systems, train deep learning computer vision models, and analyze complex graph databases. At the same time, my work in fast-paced Australian logistics, using enterprise tools like SAP Business Warehouse, has taught me the critical importance of resilience, rigorous quality assurance, and solving problems in real-time.

I believe the best software doesn't just run efficiently, it leverages data and machine learning to fundamentally improve operational workflows. I am passionate about combining my engineering expertise with my operational background to build systems that are robust, forward-thinking, and user-centric.
